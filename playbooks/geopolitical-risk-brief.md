# Geopolitical Risk Brief

## One-Line Summary

A premium information product that turns world events into actionable risk summaries for operators in exposed industries.

## Customer

- Primary buyer: importers, supply-chain consultants, executives in exposed sectors
- User: operators, analysts, founders

## Pain Point

Teams do not need more news. They need a filtered explanation of what changed and why it matters to their industry.

## Product Idea

Track relevant geopolitical stories, summarize operational impact by sector, and deliver short executive-ready briefings.

## Best APIs To Use

- [World News Intelligence](../apis/world-news-intelligence.md)
- [Supply Chain Intel](../apis/supply-chain-intel.md)

## Why This Is A Good Business

- Premium information products can command high prices in narrow niches
- Good fit for newsletters, dashboards, and advisory retainers
- The value comes from interpretation, not software complexity

## MVP Feature List

- Sector-specific watchlists
- Daily event summaries
- Impact notes by region or supplier exposure
- Weekly executive briefing

## Monetization Model

- Paid newsletter
- Subscription dashboard
- Advisory service

## Pricing Ideas

- Paid briefing: $49 to $199/month
- Team dashboard: $299+/month
- Managed intel: $2,000+/month

## Build Difficulty

Medium

## Time To First Version

1 week

## Go-To-Market Approach

- Start with one vertical such as logistics or manufacturing
- Position around executive clarity, not volume of headlines
- Build trust through concise weekly briefs

## Risks / Caveats

- The niche must be specific
- Credibility depends on clean synthesis

## Best Fit For

Paid briefing / premium intelligence product
