# Local Rank And Review Monitor

## One-Line Summary

A monitoring dashboard for local SEO agencies and multi-location businesses that tracks reviews, ratings, listing health, and nearby competitor shifts.

## Customer

- Primary buyer: local SEO agencies, franchise groups, multi-location operators
- User: account managers, owners, marketing leads

## Pain Point

Local businesses lose leads when reviews slip, competitor visibility rises, or listings become stale, but most teams notice too late.

## Product Idea

Track target businesses and competitors by city, pull review and profile data regularly, and turn it into a simple scorecard with alerts and trend summaries.

## Best APIs To Use

- [AI Google Maps Extractor](../apis/ai-google-maps-extractor.md)
- [Semrush Scraper](../apis/semrush-scraper.md)

## Why This Is A Good Business

- Agencies can sell it as a recurring reporting layer
- The output is tied to revenue and reputation
- It can start as a service and later become software

## MVP Feature List

- Track selected businesses and competitors
- Show review count, rating, and listing changes
- Flag negative trend movement
- Export a weekly client summary

## Monetization Model

- Monthly subscription
- Setup fee for onboarding locations
- Agency white-label plan

## Pricing Ideas

- Starter: $99/month for one market
- Agency: $299 to $599/month
- White-label reporting add-on: $500+ setup

## Build Difficulty

Medium

## Time To First Version

1 to 2 weeks

## Go-To-Market Approach

- Target local SEO and reputation agencies first
- Use sample reports for med spas, dentists, and law firms
- Sell a pilot before polishing the dashboard

## Risks / Caveats

- Data alone is not enough without interpretation
- Agencies may prefer exports before they want a full app

## Best Fit For
