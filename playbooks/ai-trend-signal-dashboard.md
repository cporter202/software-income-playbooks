# AI Trend Signal Dashboard

## One-Line Summary

A monitoring product that turns noisy AI trends into a cleaner weekly signal feed for founders, consultants, and creators.

## Customer

- Primary buyer: consultants, media operators, AI-focused founders
- User: analysts, content leads, founders

## Pain Point

Most AI trend coverage is either too shallow, too late, or too hype-driven to help with actual business decisions.

## Product Idea

Combine trending model, repo, and news signals into ranked summaries with practical notes on what might matter commercially.

## Best APIs To Use

- [AI Hype Tracker](../apis/ai-hype-tracker.md)
- [Reddit Scraper](../apis/reddit-scraper.md)
- [Tweets / X - Scraper](../apis/x-scraper.md)

## Why This Is A Good Business

- Can launch as a newsletter or report before becoming software
- Good for building audience and recurring revenue together
- Strong wedge for consultants and creators

## MVP Feature List

- Daily or weekly trend ingestion
- Manual or AI scoring rubric
- Short summaries with builder implications
- Archive of past signals

## Monetization Model

- Paid newsletter
- Subscription dashboard
- Consulting upsell

## Pricing Ideas

- Newsletter: $15 to $49/month
- Dashboard: $79 to $199/month
- Team intelligence tier: $299+/month

## Build Difficulty

Low to medium

## Time To First Version

3 to 10 days

## Go-To-Market Approach

- Start with a weekly memo
- Share strong summaries on X and LinkedIn
- Convert readers into paid subscribers or clients

## Risks / Caveats

- Trend products get ignored without a strong point of view
- The value comes from interpretation, not scraping volume

## Best Fit For

Newsletter / intelligence tool / micro-SaaS
