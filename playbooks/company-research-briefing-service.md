# Company Research Briefing Service

## One-Line Summary

A research workflow that turns scattered public company data into usable sales, recruiting, or strategy briefs.

## Customer

- Primary buyer: B2B agencies, consultants, recruiters
- User: account strategists, founders, recruiters

## Pain Point

Researching target companies is repetitive, slow, and inconsistent, especially when teams need briefings before sales calls, recruitment, or strategy work.

## Product Idea

Generate short company briefings that combine traffic context, search visibility, public hiring data, and role or leadership clues.

## Best APIs To Use

- [Similarweb Scraper](../apis/similarweb-scraper.md)
- [Semrush Scraper](../apis/semrush-scraper.md)
- [LinkedIn Profile Scraper](../apis/linkedin-profile-scraper.md)

## Why This Is A Good Business

- High perceived value for agencies and consultants
- Easy to sell as a service before packaging software
- Can evolve into reusable internal tooling

## MVP Feature List

- Company URL or name intake
- Pull traffic and SEO context
- Pull relevant profile context
- Output a one-page briefing

## Monetization Model

- Per-brief fee
- Monthly research retainer
- Internal team subscription

## Pricing Ideas

- Per brief: $50 to $200
- Team retainer: $500 to $3,000/month
- Self-serve SaaS: $79 to $249/month

## Build Difficulty

Low to medium

## Time To First Version

3 to 7 days

## Go-To-Market Approach

- Sell to agencies that already need pre-call research
- Package five briefings as an easy entry offer
- Use manual delivery to discover what sections matter most

## Risks / Caveats

- The output must be concise enough to be used
- Without a strong template, research products can feel generic

## Best Fit For

Productized service / internal tool / SaaS
