# Creator Sponsorship Prospector

## One-Line Summary

A sponsorship research workflow that helps creator agencies and consultants build better-fit brand prospect lists faster.

## Customer

- Primary buyer: creator agencies, sponsorship consultants, media operators
- User: partnerships managers, talent managers

## Pain Point

Sponsorship teams spend too much time assembling weak brand lists and too little time on tailored outreach.

## Product Idea

Generate sponsor shortlists by niche, industry, and creator profile, then enrich them into outreach-ready opportunities.

## Best APIs To Use

- [Find Top Sponsors](../apis/find-top-sponsors.md)
- [Tweets / X - Scraper](../apis/x-scraper.md)

## Why This Is A Good Business

- Can be sold immediately as a service
- Useful for agencies before it is polished enough for self-serve
- The buyer values speed and fit more than software depth

## MVP Feature List

- Creator niche intake
- Sponsor shortlist generation
- Relevance notes per brand
- Export-ready outreach list

## Monetization Model

- Per-list fee
- Monthly sponsorship prospecting retainer
- Agency dashboard subscription

## Pricing Ideas

- One-time list: $200 to $750
- Monthly retainer: $750 to $3,000
- SaaS plan: $99 to $299/month

## Build Difficulty

Low to medium

## Time To First Version

3 to 7 days

## Go-To-Market Approach

- Target boutique creator agencies first
- Offer a sample sponsor shortlist for one creator
- Package it as a high-speed research layer

## Risks / Caveats

- Research quality matters more than raw list size
- Best results come from strong niche specialization

## Best Fit For

Productized service / agency workflow
