# Social Listening Alert System

## One-Line Summary

A monitoring tool that alerts brands, agencies, and research teams when important conversations or narrative shifts appear in public social channels.

## Customer

- Primary buyer: agencies, brand teams, consultants
- User: strategists, researchers, community leads

## Pain Point

Teams cannot manually keep up with fast-moving conversations across platforms, so they miss threats, trends, and demand signals.

## Product Idea

Track keywords, competitors, or topics across public discussions and summarize notable changes into daily or weekly alerts.

## Best APIs To Use

- [Tweets / X - Scraper](../apis/x-scraper.md)
- [Reddit Scraper](../apis/reddit-scraper.md)

## Why This Is A Good Business

- Useful for internal teams and agencies
- Easy to sell as a reporting layer first
- Strong recurring value when alerts are trusted

## MVP Feature List

- Topic watchlists
- Daily mention collection
- Basic sentiment or narrative tagging
- Email or Slack-style summary

## Monetization Model

- Team subscription
- Agency reporting add-on
- Research retainer

## Pricing Ideas

- Starter: $49/month
- Team: $149 to $399/month
- Managed monitoring: $1,000+/month

## Build Difficulty

Low to medium

## Time To First Version

3 to 10 days

## Go-To-Market Approach

- Sell to agencies already producing reports
- Offer trial monitoring for one keyword set
- Emphasize saved research time and faster response

## Risks / Caveats

- Noise can overwhelm the product without good filtering
- Buyers need summaries and action cues, not raw feeds

## Best Fit For

Internal tool / agency service / SaaS
