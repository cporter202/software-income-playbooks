# Competitor Traffic Watchtower

## One-Line Summary

A competitor intelligence product that tracks traffic and search visibility shifts for agencies, founders, and growth teams.

## Customer

- Primary buyer: SEO agencies, growth consultants, in-house marketers
- User: strategists, founders, account managers

## Pain Point

Teams know they should watch competitors, but manual checks are inconsistent and usually happen after performance drops.

## Product Idea

Monitor a shortlist of competitor domains and generate simple weekly changes in traffic, rankings, and notable movement.

## Best APIs To Use

- [Similarweb Scraper](../apis/similarweb-scraper.md)
- [Semrush Scraper](../apis/semrush-scraper.md)

## Why This Is A Good Business

- The output is easy to package into retainers
- Buyers care about shifts, not raw data dumps
- It can support both agency and self-serve models

## MVP Feature List

- Domain watchlists
- Weekly movement reports
- Top winner and loser summaries
- Simple recommendations or flags

## Monetization Model

- Monthly subscription
- Agency client add-on
- Premium alert plan

## Pricing Ideas

- Solo: $59/month
- Team: $199/month
- Agency reporting package: $500+/month

## Build Difficulty

Medium

## Time To First Version

3 to 10 days

## Go-To-Market Approach

- Start with SEO and content agencies
- Publish anonymized sample reports
- Use one niche, such as legal or e-commerce, for your first wedge

## Risks / Caveats

- Generic dashboards are easy to ignore
- The product needs a clear narrative or alerting layer

## Best Fit For

Agency service / micro-SaaS
