# Grant And Compliance Radar

## One-Line Summary

A monitoring product that helps niche operators spot funding opportunities and compliance-relevant signals without manually watching fragmented sources.

## Customer

- Primary buyer: grant consultants, nonprofits, regulated businesses
- User: founders, operations teams, funding advisors

## Pain Point

Teams miss grants and important compliance changes because the research burden is repetitive and scattered across many sources.

## Product Idea

Let users follow specific sectors or eligibility themes, then send a filtered stream of funding and regulatory changes with short takeaways.

## Best APIs To Use

- [Funding Intel](../apis/funding-intel.md)
- [AI Company Researcher](../apis/ai-company-researcher.md)

## Why This Is A Good Business

- Strong recurring reason to stay subscribed
- Easy to start as a niche service
- Valuable to consultants managing multiple clients

## MVP Feature List

- Topic or sector watchlists
- Opportunity summaries
- Eligibility notes
- Weekly digest output

## Monetization Model

- Monthly subscription
- Consultant team plan
- Done-for-you research retainer

## Pricing Ideas

- Solo: $49 to $149/month
- Consultant plan: $249/month
- Managed research: $1,000+/month

## Build Difficulty

Medium

## Time To First Version

1 week

## Go-To-Market Approach

- Start with one vertical like climate, manufacturing, or nonprofits
- Sell time savings and missed-opportunity reduction
- Offer a curated weekly memo first

## Risks / Caveats

- Needs tight market focus to stay useful
- Users expect relevance, not a generic feed

## Best Fit For

SaaS / productized service
