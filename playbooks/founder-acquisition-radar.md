# Founder Acquisition Radar

## One-Line Summary

A startup acquisition watchlist that helps buyers and brokers monitor new listings, valuation patterns, and niche opportunities.

## Customer

- Primary buyer: acquisition entrepreneurs, holdcos, brokers
- User: deal sourcers, operators, analysts

## Pain Point

Deal teams waste time manually scanning marketplaces and lack a clean workflow for comparing opportunities over time.

## Product Idea

Track startup listings, tag them by niche and business model, and surface notable opportunities or shifts in the market.

## Best APIs To Use

- [Acquire.com Scraper](../apis/acquire-com-scraper.md)
- [Similarweb Scraper](../apis/similarweb-scraper.md)

## Why This Is A Good Business

- Small audience, but high-value buyers
- Strong fit for subscription plus advisory upsells
- Valuable even in a narrow vertical

## MVP Feature List

- New listing feed
- Filters by niche, size, and business model
- Watchlists for buyers
- Weekly market summary

## Monetization Model

- Premium subscription
- Broker research service
- High-touch advisory package

## Pricing Ideas

- Watchlist product: $149 to $399/month
- Broker dashboard: $500+/month
- Bespoke sourcing: $2,000+/month

## Build Difficulty

Medium

## Time To First Version

1 week

## Go-To-Market Approach

- Target acquisition entrepreneurs first
- Publish a focused weekly memo on a niche like micro-SaaS
- Offer custom sourcing as a premium tier

## Risks / Caveats

- The niche is smaller than general startup tooling
- Premium positioning matters more than feature count

## Best Fit For

Data product / advisory workflow
