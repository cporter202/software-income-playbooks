# Amazon Seller Gap Monitor

## One-Line Summary

A monitoring tool that helps Amazon operators spot weak sellers, listing gaps, and review-driven product opportunities.

## Customer

- Primary buyer: Amazon brands, marketplace agencies, aggregators
- User: operators, marketplace analysts, founders

## Pain Point

Amazon teams spend hours manually comparing sellers, reading reviews, and hunting for openings that could drive profitable product decisions.

## Product Idea

Track selected categories or competitors, surface weak seller signals, and summarize review themes that indicate unmet demand.

## Best APIs To Use

- [Amazon Seller Data Extractor](../apis/amazon-seller-data-extractor.md)
- [Reddit Scraper](../apis/reddit-scraper.md)

## Why This Is A Good Business

- E-commerce operators already pay for better research
- Opportunity reports can be sold before software
- It creates recurring value when used as a watchlist

## MVP Feature List

- Seller watchlists
- Category and competitor snapshots
- Review-theme summaries
- Opportunity score by seller or niche

## Monetization Model

- Subscription
- Category report package
- Consulting upsell

## Pricing Ideas

- Starter: $99/month
- Team: $299/month
- Research package: $500 to $2,000

## Build Difficulty

Medium

## Time To First Version

1 to 2 weeks

## Go-To-Market Approach

- Target consultants and operators in one product category
- Lead with sample competitor reports
- Turn one-off analyses into a recurring watchlist

## Risks / Caveats

- Too much raw data can overwhelm buyers
- The tool needs clear scoring and interpretation

## Best Fit For

SaaS / analyst tool
