# SEO Opportunity Briefing

## One-Line Summary

A report product that shows businesses where they can realistically win more search demand with lower-friction content and keyword targets.

## Customer

- Primary buyer: SEO agencies, niche site builders, SaaS founders
- User: strategists, founders, content leads

## Pain Point

Teams waste time chasing broad SEO opportunities instead of identifying the smaller keyword clusters that can produce faster wins.

## Product Idea

Generate focused SEO opportunity briefs with keyword clusters, competitor context, and next-best topics tied to likely revenue outcomes.

## Best APIs To Use

- [Keyword Opportunity Agent](../apis/keyword-opportunity-agent.md)
- [Semrush Scraper](../apis/semrush-scraper.md)

## Why This Is A Good Business

- Can be sold immediately as a service
- Converts easily into recurring planning and tracking
- Gives agencies a strong foot-in-the-door offer

## MVP Feature List

- Keyword opportunity clusters
- Competitive gap summary
- Priority topics and pages
- PDF or Markdown briefing export

## Monetization Model

- One-time strategy brief
- Monthly retained planning
- SaaS dashboard for agencies

## Pricing Ideas

- Single brief: $200 to $800
- Monthly planning: $500 to $2,000
- SaaS: $49 to $199/month

## Build Difficulty

Low to medium

## Time To First Version

3 to 7 days

## Go-To-Market Approach

- Sell into agencies and consultants first
- Use vertical examples like legal, dental, or SaaS
- Position around speed to first SEO win

## Risks / Caveats

- SEO promises must stay credible
- The best version narrows to one niche, not all SEO

## Best Fit For

Productized service / SaaS
