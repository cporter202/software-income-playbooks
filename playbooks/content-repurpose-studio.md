# Content Repurpose Studio

## One-Line Summary

A content automation workflow that turns long-form video into ready-to-publish written assets for creators, consultants, and B2B brands.

## Customer

- Primary buyer: creators, agencies, B2B marketing teams
- User: content managers, founders, ghostwriters

## Pain Point

Teams publish long-form content but fail to convert it into multiple channel assets because the manual repurposing work is slow and inconsistent.

## Product Idea

Pull transcripts from videos, summarize the best ideas, and output drafts for LinkedIn posts, emails, threads, or internal notes.

## Best APIs To Use

- [Youtube Transcript Scraper](../apis/youtube-transcript-scraper.md)
- [Reddit Scraper](../apis/reddit-scraper.md)

## Why This Is A Good Business

- Easy to start as a service
- Clear output buyers can see and judge immediately
- Fast MVP with low infrastructure overhead

## MVP Feature List

- Video URL intake
- Transcript extraction
- Content asset templates
- Export to Markdown or Google Docs

## Monetization Model

- Per-video fee
- Monthly content package
- SaaS subscription for self-serve repurposing

## Pricing Ideas

- Per asset batch: $50 to $250
- Monthly package: $500 to $2,000
- Self-serve tool: $29 to $149/month

## Build Difficulty

Low

## Time To First Version

3 to 7 days

## Go-To-Market Approach

- Start with consultants and podcasters
- Show before/after examples from existing videos
- Use service delivery to learn which outputs matter most

## Risks / Caveats

- Generic AI copy is easy to replace
- You need good templates and editing standards to feel premium

## Best Fit For

Automation service / SaaS
