# Recruiting Market Radar

## One-Line Summary

A hiring intelligence dashboard that helps recruiters and talent teams monitor job demand, employer activity, and talent hotspots.

## Customer

- Primary buyer: recruiting firms, internal talent teams, sourcing leaders
- User: recruiters, recruiting ops, founders hiring in volume

## Pain Point

Recruiters often work from anecdote instead of real market signal when deciding where to source, pitch, or specialize.

## Product Idea

Track jobs by role, company, and region, then combine that with company growth signals to show where hiring demand is rising or cooling.

## Best APIs To Use

- [Linkedin Jobs Scraper](../apis/linkedin-jobs-scraper.md)
- [LinkedIn Profile Scraper](../apis/linkedin-profile-scraper.md)
- [Similarweb Scraper](../apis/similarweb-scraper.md)

## Why This Is A Good Business

- Recruiters pay for time-saving intelligence
- The output can be delivered weekly
- It creates a path from report product to software

## MVP Feature List

- Track selected role keywords
- Show hiring counts by company and city
- Flag employers with rising demand
- Export a weekly recruiter brief

## Monetization Model

- Team subscription
- Per-niche report package
- Agency intelligence retainer

## Pricing Ideas

- Solo recruiter: $79/month
- Agency team: $249 to $499/month
- Custom reporting: $1,000+/month

## Build Difficulty

Medium

## Time To First Version

1 to 2 weeks

## Go-To-Market Approach

- Focus on one niche like B2B SaaS sales or AI engineering
- Share sample labor snapshots on LinkedIn
- Offer pilot reports to recruiting firms

## Risks / Caveats

- Recruiters need signal quality more than raw volume
- Tight scope matters or the product becomes a generic job board clone

## Best Fit For

SaaS / internal tool / recruiting ops product
