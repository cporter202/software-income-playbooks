# Review Recovery Engine

## One-Line Summary

A local-business intelligence product that turns reviews into clear action items, messaging fixes, and service recovery priorities.

## Customer

- Primary buyer: local business owners, reputation agencies, franchise operators
- User: operators, marketers, account managers

## Pain Point

Businesses know bad reviews hurt growth, but they rarely have a systematic way to diagnose patterns and turn feedback into action.

## Product Idea

Ingest Google Maps reviews, summarize recurring complaints and praise, cluster issues by theme, and surface the top fixes that could move reputation and conversions.

## Best APIs To Use

- [Google Maps AI Reviews Analyzer](../apis/google-maps-ai-reviews-analyzer.md)
- [AI Google Maps Extractor](../apis/ai-google-maps-extractor.md)

## Why This Is A Good Business

- Easy to sell as a report or monthly service
- Strong tie to revenue and lead conversion
- Works for agencies before it becomes software

## MVP Feature List

- Review import and trend summary
- Complaint and praise clustering
- Competitor comparison
- Action-plan export for owners or agencies

## Monetization Model

- One-time audit
- Monthly monitoring
- White-label agency reporting

## Pricing Ideas

- Audit: $250 to $1,000
- Monthly monitoring: $99 to $499/month
- Agency tier: $500+/month

## Build Difficulty

Low to medium

## Time To First Version

3 to 7 days

## Go-To-Market Approach

- Target med spas, dentists, and restaurants
- Use before-and-after examples from review themes
- Sell the audit first, then convert into monitoring

## Risks / Caveats

- Generic sentiment summaries are weak
- The product needs specific operational recommendations

## Best Fit For

Agency service / micro-SaaS
