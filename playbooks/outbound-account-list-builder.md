# Outbound Account List Builder

## One-Line Summary

A lean prospecting product that generates filtered B2B account lists with contact and profile context for a narrow market.

## Customer

- Primary buyer: lead gen agencies, SDR teams, outbound consultants
- User: founders, SDRs, agency operators

## Pain Point

Most outbound teams waste time stitching together messy lists from multiple tools before they can even start outreach.

## Product Idea

Let users define a niche, geography, company traits, and decision-maker targets, then produce a cleaner list with contact and company context.

## Best APIs To Use

- [Leads Finder](../apis/leads-finder.md)
- [LinkedIn Profile Scraper](../apis/linkedin-profile-scraper.md)

## Why This Is A Good Business

- Easy to sell as an output, not a platform
- Works as a service before becoming SaaS
- Buyers feel the pain weekly, not quarterly

## MVP Feature List

- Intake form for niche and geography
- Lead pull and profile enrichment
- Scoring rules for best-fit accounts
- CSV export plus short research notes

## Monetization Model

- Per-list fee
- Monthly retained list service
- Seat-based SaaS for agencies

## Pricing Ideas

- One-time list: $150 to $500
- Monthly list service: $500 to $2,000
- SaaS plan: $79 to $249/month

## Build Difficulty

Low to medium

## Time To First Version

3 to 7 days

## Go-To-Market Approach

- Pick one vertical such as recruiting agencies or dev shops
- Sell the first version as a done-for-you deliverable
- Use case studies built from your own outbound campaigns

## Risks / Caveats

- Commodity risk if there is no niche or scoring layer
- Data freshness and lead quality need constant review

## Best Fit For

Productized service / SaaS
