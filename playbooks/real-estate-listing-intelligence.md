# Real Estate Listing Intelligence

## One-Line Summary

A listing watchlist product that helps investors and broker teams track target markets, new listings, and pricing movement.

## Customer

- Primary buyer: investors, wholesalers, broker teams
- User: analysts, acquisition teams, operators

## Pain Point

Real estate teams manually monitor markets and lose time piecing together listing changes, neighborhood context, and opportunity signals.

## Product Idea

Track selected geographies and property filters, then surface meaningful changes in price, listing velocity, and nearby signals.

## Best APIs To Use

- [Zillow Free Scraper](../apis/zillow-free-scraper.md)
- [AI Google Maps Extractor](../apis/ai-google-maps-extractor.md)

## Why This Is A Good Business

- Strong buyer urgency in active markets
- Easy to productize for a specific niche
- Can support both subscription and services

## MVP Feature List

- Saved market watchlists
- Listing change alerts
- Neighborhood context snapshots
- Exportable property brief

## Monetization Model

- Subscription by market or seat
- Investor research service
- Broker team package

## Pricing Ideas

- Solo investor: $79/month
- Team plan: $249/month
- Custom market report: $500+

## Build Difficulty

Medium

## Time To First Version

1 to 2 weeks

## Go-To-Market Approach

- Start with wholesalers or investor groups in one metro
- Share sample market snapshots
- Position it as a faster watchlist, not a general listing portal

## Risks / Caveats

- Real estate products need a tight geographic wedge
- Alert quality matters more than dashboard breadth

## Best Fit For

Lead gen asset / SaaS
