# LinkedIn Ad Intelligence Vault

## One-Line Summary

A B2B ad research product that lets agencies and marketers track competitor LinkedIn ad creatives, positioning, and campaign shifts.

## Customer

- Primary buyer: B2B growth teams, paid media agencies, consultants
- User: strategists, copywriters, founders

## Pain Point

B2B marketers need competitor ad insight, but compiling campaigns manually is slow and inconsistent.

## Product Idea

Track a set of companies, scrape their LinkedIn ads, and organize the output into a searchable vault with change alerts and messaging patterns.

## Best APIs To Use

- [LinkedIn Ads Scraper](../apis/linkedin-ads-scraper.md)
- [LinkedIn Post Scraper](../apis/linkedin-post-scraper.md)

## Why This Is A Good Business

- Clear agency and operator buyer
- High-value premium research product
- Strong fit for recurring subscriptions and retainers

## MVP Feature List

- Competitor watchlists
- Ad archive with filters
- Messaging and CTA pattern summaries
- Weekly change digest

## Monetization Model

- Subscription
- Team seats
- Agency research retainers

## Pricing Ideas

- Solo: $79/month
- Team: $249 to $599/month
- Managed intelligence: $1,000+/month

## Build Difficulty

Medium

## Time To First Version

1 week

## Go-To-Market Approach

- Focus on one B2B niche like RevOps or HR tech
- Publish anonymized creative trend reports
- Lead with "see how competitors are positioning this quarter"

## Risks / Caveats

- The vault needs smart organization, not just data
- Broad ad databases can become noise quickly

## Best Fit For

SaaS / agency intelligence product
