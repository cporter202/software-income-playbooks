# Hospitality Reputation Monitor

## One-Line Summary

A review intelligence product for hotels, restaurants, and attractions that turns public feedback into clear operational and marketing insights.

## Customer

- Primary buyer: hospitality operators, hotel groups, restaurant consultants
- User: managers, operators, marketers

## Pain Point

Hospitality teams are drowning in reviews but still lack a clear view of what is driving poor experiences or better-performing competitors.

## Product Idea

Aggregate review data, benchmark similar properties, and surface operational issues, praise themes, and brand perception patterns.

## Best APIs To Use

- [Tripadvisor Reviews](../apis/tripadvisor-reviews.md)
- [Google Maps AI Reviews Analyzer](../apis/google-maps-ai-reviews-analyzer.md)

## Why This Is A Good Business

- Strong pain with measurable business impact
- Works well as a premium reporting layer
- Easy path from consulting to software

## MVP Feature List

- Property watchlists
- Theme clustering across reviews
- Competitor benchmarks
- Management-ready summary report

## Monetization Model

- Monthly subscription
- Multi-location reporting
- Hospitality consulting upsell

## Pricing Ideas

- Single property: $79/month
- Group plan: $299+/month
- Managed reporting: $1,000+/month

## Build Difficulty

Medium

## Time To First Version

1 week

## Go-To-Market Approach

- Start with boutique hotel consultants or restaurant groups
- Sell a review benchmark report first
- Use one hospitality sub-niche before expanding

## Risks / Caveats

- Hospitality buyers want clear actions, not general sentiment
- The product must feel operational, not vague

## Best Fit For

SaaS / agency service
