# Supplier Risk Watchtower

## One-Line Summary

A risk-monitoring product for importers and operators that flags supply-chain disruptions before they turn into expensive surprises.

## Customer

- Primary buyer: importers, manufacturers, procurement teams
- User: operations leads, sourcing managers, founders

## Pain Point

Teams hear about disruptions too late and scramble after shipping, tariff, or geopolitical problems are already hurting margins.

## Product Idea

Track regions, ports, suppliers, and relevant news themes, then deliver concise alerts and weekly risk summaries.

## Best APIs To Use

- [Supply Chain Intel](../apis/supply-chain-intel.md)
- [World News Intelligence](../apis/world-news-intelligence.md)

## Why This Is A Good Business

- The underlying problem is expensive and recurring
- Buyers will pay for concise intelligence that saves time
- Strong fit for premium B2B subscriptions

## MVP Feature List

- Risk watchlists by region or topic
- Daily or weekly summaries
- Escalation alerts for critical shifts
- Executive-style report export

## Monetization Model

- Premium subscription
- Team plan
- Advisory service upsell

## Pricing Ideas

- Starter: $149/month
- Team: $499/month
- Managed briefings: $1,500+/month

## Build Difficulty

Medium

## Time To First Version

1 to 2 weeks

## Go-To-Market Approach

- Target one industry like furniture importers or industrial parts
- Lead with a weekly brief rather than a full dashboard
- Sell clarity and speed, not "all data in one place"

## Risks / Caveats

- Broad risk monitoring gets noisy fast
- Actionability matters more than signal volume

## Best Fit For

SaaS / premium research product
