# Travel Price Watch Concierge

## One-Line Summary

A route-monitoring product that helps founders, executives, and agencies save money on frequent travel by tracking specific flights and fare shifts.

## Customer

- Primary buyer: frequent business travelers, travel concierges, executive assistants
- User: founders, operators, assistants

## Pain Point

People who fly often on repeat routes rarely have a disciplined way to monitor price changes and end up overpaying.

## Product Idea

Watch saved routes and dates, summarize fare movement, and send simple book-now or wait alerts.

## Best APIs To Use

- [Google Flights Smart Scraper](../apis/google-flights-smart-scraper.md)

## Why This Is A Good Business

- Clear consumer and SMB value
- Useful as a concierge service before full software
- Alert-based workflows are simple to understand

## MVP Feature List

- Saved route watchlists
- Daily fare checks
- Book-now or wait recommendations
- Email summary output

## Monetization Model

- Subscription
- Concierge service
- Executive assistant team plan

## Pricing Ideas

- Solo: $19 to $49/month
- Concierge: $99 to $299/month
- Team plan: $199+/month

## Build Difficulty

Low to medium

## Time To First Version

3 to 7 days

## Go-To-Market Approach

- Start with founders flying between the same cities often
- Market it as a time and cost-saving assistant
- Offer managed setup for route lists

## Risks / Caveats

- Broad travel deals are noisy
- The best version is route-specific and repeatable

## Best Fit For

Automation / concierge service / micro-SaaS
