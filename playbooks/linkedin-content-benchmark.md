# LinkedIn Content Benchmark

## One-Line Summary

A B2B content intelligence product that shows founders and marketers what posting patterns are actually driving attention in their category.

## Customer

- Primary buyer: startup founders, B2B marketers, ghostwriting agencies
- User: founders, content leads, strategists

## Pain Point

Most teams guess at LinkedIn content strategy instead of learning from what top accounts in their niche are doing.

## Product Idea

Track a niche set of people and companies, compare their posting styles and engagement patterns, and produce benchmark summaries with practical suggestions.

## Best APIs To Use

- [LinkedIn Post Scraper](../apis/linkedin-post-scraper.md)
- [LinkedIn Profile Scraper](../apis/linkedin-profile-scraper.md)

## Why This Is A Good Business

- Very easy to sell as a report product
- Strong agency and ghostwriter wedge
- Simple first version can be manual-plus-automation

## MVP Feature List

- Watchlist creation
- Engagement and posting frequency summaries
- Topic and format tagging
- Weekly benchmark email

## Monetization Model

- Monthly subscription
- Ghostwriter research package
- Agency dashboard

## Pricing Ideas

- Solo creator: $39/month
- Agency plan: $149 to $399/month
- Research package: $300 to $1,000

## Build Difficulty

Low to medium

## Time To First Version

3 to 7 days

## Go-To-Market Approach

- Start with ghostwriters and founder-led agencies
- Publish niche benchmark examples
- Position around content clarity, not vanity metrics

## Risks / Caveats

- Engagement benchmarks need category context
- Broad "social media analytics" positioning will dilute the offer

## Best Fit For

Productized service / SaaS
