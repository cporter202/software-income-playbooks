# Account Intel Copilot

## One-Line Summary

A pre-call and pre-outreach research tool that builds concise account briefs for agencies, consultants, and B2B sales teams.

## Customer

- Primary buyer: agencies, consultants, SDR teams
- User: founders, AEs, strategists

## Pain Point

Too much sales and consulting outreach is generic because people do not have time to research every target account properly.

## Product Idea

Generate a short account brief with company context, leadership notes, recent public activity, and directional market signals before outreach or discovery calls.

## Best APIs To Use

- [AI Company Researcher](../apis/ai-company-researcher.md)
- [LinkedIn Profile Scraper](../apis/linkedin-profile-scraper.md)
- [Similarweb Scraper](../apis/similarweb-scraper.md)

## Why This Is A Good Business

- Strong immediate value in services and sales
- Easy to deliver manually before productizing
- Can become embedded in operating workflows

## MVP Feature List

- Company research intake
- Leadership summary
- Traffic and hiring context
- One-page outreach brief

## Monetization Model

- Per-brief
- Monthly team plan
- Agency internal tooling subscription

## Pricing Ideas

- Per brief: $25 to $100
- Team plan: $79 to $249/month
- Done-for-you research: $500+/month

## Build Difficulty

Low to medium

## Time To First Version

3 to 7 days

## Go-To-Market Approach

- Sell to agencies that already prep for calls manually
- Package five briefs as a test offer
- Use output quality and speed as the hook

## Risks / Caveats

- Briefs must be concise and credible
- Generic summaries will get ignored

## Best Fit For

Internal tool / productized service / SaaS
