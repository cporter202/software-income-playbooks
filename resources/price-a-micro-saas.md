# How To Price A Micro-SaaS

Pricing should reflect business value, not just API cost.

## Start With The Use Case

- A lead generation tool can be priced by output volume or seat
- A monitoring tool can be priced by tracked entities or alert volume
- A reporting tool can be priced by report frequency or client count
- A service-backed product can include setup, onboarding, and interpretation

## Practical Pricing Shapes

- Low-touch automation: $29 to $99 per month
- Team workflow tool: $99 to $399 per month
- Agency-facing tool: $149 to $750 per month
- Done-for-you reporting service: $300 to $2,500 per month

## Pricing Mistakes

- Matching API cost instead of customer value
- Hiding onboarding or setup work inside a low monthly plan
- Launching with too many tiers
- Trying to price for enterprise before proving the niche

## A Better Starting Approach

1. Price the service version first.
2. Identify what part becomes software.
3. Keep one core plan and one higher-touch option.
4. Raise prices when the workflow becomes part of the customer's operating rhythm.
